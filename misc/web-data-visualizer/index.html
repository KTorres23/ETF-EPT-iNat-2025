About the ETF-EPT-iNat-2025 Visualizer

This web visualizer was made for visualizing and exploring the ETF-EPT-iNat-2025 dataset, which curates community-contributed observations of three aquatic insect orders—Ephemeroptera (mayflies), Plecoptera (stoneflies), and Trichoptera (caddisflies)—from iNaturalist.

Target Indicator Orders

Ephemeroptera (Mayflies)

Short-lived winged adults with aquatic nymphs featuring abdominal gills. Key bioindicators of organic stream health.

Plecoptera (Stoneflies)

Require clean, highly oxygenated cold running waters. Highly sensitive to water pollution and sedimentation.

Trichoptera (Caddisflies)

Famous for constructing intricate protective portable silk cases from gravel or plant debris.
